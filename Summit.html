    Drag the place cards, or use the arrows, then seat them.

    How to play

    Each day the Office of Protocol names one statistic. Put the delegations in order, seat I at the head of the table. You have three attempts. Correctly seated delegations turn green and stay in place.

    On Mondays and Fridays an Extraordinary Summit brings seven delegations with closer figures.

    Figures are rounded estimates from around 2023 (UN, World Bank). "Year founded" means the year of independence or unification of the modern state; countries with no clear founding date sit those rounds out.
